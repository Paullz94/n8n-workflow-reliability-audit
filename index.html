Why this exists

Most automation failures are not “node is red” failures.

Duplicate side effects

A retry can create a second invoice, ticket, row, message or CRM mutation unless the workflow is intentionally idempotent.

Silent partial success

One branch may complete while another fails, leaving downstream systems inconsistent even though the workflow looks mostly healthy.

Recovery nobody tested

Retries, error workflows and manual recovery often exist on paper but have never been proven against a synthetic failure.
