# PCFlows Sales Playbook

First minimum validation milestone: **EUR 1,000 in settled external-customer gross revenue.** This is not a stop condition.

## Launch state

Pre-launch B2B acquisition may run now when clearly labeled as pre-launch. Paid service, payment acceptance and invoicing begin no earlier than **2026-10-01**.

Do not send prospects to an active checkout before the commercial start gate is complete.

## Ideal customer profile

Prioritize organizations that already use n8n, run workflows with irreversible or externally visible side effects, depend on APIs/webhooks/CRM/billing/email/databases/support systems, and have a clear operational owner and measurable acceptance condition.

Strong signals include duplicate processing, unreliable webhooks, manual replay after partial failure, unclear retry behavior, missing error paths, data-integrity concerns and production automation with little test evidence.

## Offer ladder

### Focused Risk Check — EUR 79
One sanitized n8n workflow export and one agreed reliability risk area. Evidence-backed findings and bounded remediation guidance. No included re-scan.

### Data Integrity Audit — EUR 149
One sanitized n8n workflow export. Broader reliability/data-integrity review, prioritized remediation and verification guidance, plus one asynchronous re-scan.

### Portfolio / Release QA — EUR 399
Up to three related sanitized n8n workflow exports. Combined release-risk view and one combined re-scan round.

## Qualification

Accept only when business outcome, connected systems, current symptom/risk, n8n version/hosting, approximate node count, acceptance condition and sanitization are clear.

Reject or rescope requests for public credentials/data, guarantees, certification/regulatory sign-off, unlimited revisions or broad production access.

## Outreach rules

Pre-launch acquisition may run before 2026-10-01, but messages must clearly state that PCFlows paid services start no earlier than 1 October 2026.

Prefer relevant legal-entity contact routes such as generic business addresses and public contact forms. Do not continue after opt-out. Keep outreach narrow, relevant and non-deceptive.

Never use fake personalization, fake case studies, fabricated urgency or misleading claims.

## Revenue tracking

Count only settled Stripe payments from unrelated external customers. Do not count test payments, owner-funded payments, pending/failed payments, refunds, unpaid requests, GitHub traffic or compliments.

EUR 1,000 verified gross is the first minimum validation milestone only. Continue growth after it.

## Operator behavior

The autonomous operator should monitor the PCFlows inbox, customer-relevant GitHub state and Stripe; keep the Inbox Operator as the sole customer-email responder; keep the B2B Pipeline as owner of outbound acquisition; reconcile real external payments before fulfillment; keep secrets/customer data out of public GitHub; and escalate only genuine owner-only legal/KYC/credential/spend actions.
