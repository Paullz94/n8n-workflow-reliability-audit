Evidence-first methodology

What the audit knows — and what it does not.

PCFlows starts with deterministic analysis of a sanitized Make.com blueprint. The goal is not to award a vague health score. The goal is to identify concrete reliability conditions and define a safe way to verify them.

01

Read the blueprint

Inspect exported structure, scenario settings, module types and error routes. Production credentials are not required.

02

Apply Make semantics

Rules are tied to documented Make behavior where possible. A finding is a review signal, not proof that production is broken.

03

Prioritize with context

The paid audit uses only a small allowlisted context file: scenario goal, critical side effects, duplicate tolerance, ordering and recovery expectations.

04

Verify safely

Important findings receive concrete verification steps using sanitized or synthetic test events.

Signals currently reviewed

Retry
Can repeating a write repeat the external side effect?
Skip / Ignore
Can a failed bundle disappear while later work continues?
Resume
Can substitute output make downstream processing look healthier than the failed write really was?
Commit / Rollback
Can an error leave partial state, or is rollback limited by work already committed?
Concurrency
Can overlapping runs race on the same business record?
Recovery
Is failed work visible and recoverable under the exported scenario settings?
Filters
Can a filtered-out bundle look like technical success while the expected business write never happened?
Observability
Do confidentiality settings reduce the evidence available during incident diagnosis?

Deterministic first. AI second.

The free preflight is deterministic. For the paid audit, AI-assisted reasoning may be used only on a privacy-bounded packet containing deterministic findings and allowlisted business context. That review packet does not contain the raw blueprint.

AI may help prioritize, explain ambiguity and suggest safe verification. It is not allowed to invent runtime incidents, credentials, logs or unsupported customer impact.

Static-analysis limits

  • No blueprint can prove that credentials are valid.
  • It cannot prove that a third-party API is currently healthy.
  • It cannot know complete business intent without customer context.
  • It cannot prove that every defect has been found.
  • It is not a security, legal, accounting or compliance certification.
