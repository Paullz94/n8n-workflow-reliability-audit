Evidence-first methodology

What the audit knows — and what it does not.

PCFlows starts with deterministic analysis of a sanitized Make.com blueprint. The goal is not to award a vague health score. The goal is to identify concrete reliability conditions, explain why they may matter to business data, and define the smallest safe way to verify them.

1 · Evidence

Read the blueprint

PCFlows inspects exported structure, scenario settings, module types, error routes and selected non-sensitive metadata. It does not need production credentials.

2 · Interpret

Apply Make semantics

Rules are tied to documented Make behavior where possible. A finding is a review signal, not proof that production is broken.

3 · Prioritize

Use business context

The paid audit uses a small allowlisted context file: scenario goal, critical side effects, duplicate tolerance, ordering and recovery expectations.

4 · Verify

Design safe tests

Each important finding gets a concrete verification step using sanitized or synthetic test events rather than production secrets.

Reliability signals currently reviewed

Retry & idempotencyCan retrying a write repeat an external side effect?
Skip / Ignore handlersCan a failed bundle be dropped while the scenario continues?
Resume handlersCan substitute output make downstream processing look healthier than the failed write really was?
Commit / Rollback boundariesCan an error leave intentional partial state, or is rollback limited by earlier commits?
Concurrency & orderingCan overlapping runs race on the same business record?
Incomplete executions & data lossIs failed work recoverable according to the exported scenario settings?
Silent filter skipsCan the scenario finish without the expected business-side write ever occurring?
ObservabilityDo privacy/confidentiality settings reduce the evidence available during incident diagnosis?

Deterministic first, AI second

The free preflight is deterministic. For the paid audit, AI-assisted reasoning may be used only on a privacy-bounded packet containing the deterministic findings and allowlisted business context. It does not receive the raw blueprint through this review packet.

AI may help prioritize, explain ambiguity and propose safe verification. It is not allowed to invent runtime incidents, credentials, logs or unsupported customer impact.

Static-analysis limits

  • No static blueprint can prove that credentials are valid.
  • It cannot prove that a third-party API is currently healthy.
  • It cannot know whether business intent is correct unless the customer provides context.
  • It cannot prove that every defect has been found.
  • It is not a security, legal, accounting or compliance certification.
