Frequently asked questions

Do you need access to my Make.com account?

No. The current audit is designed around a sanitized exported scenario blueprint. Production credentials are not required.

Does the free scanner upload my blueprint?

No. The core Reliability Preflight runs locally in your browser and does not upload the blueprint to PCFlows.

What should I remove before sharing a blueprint?

Remove API keys, tokens, passwords, private webhook secrets, personal data and other confidential literals. Never send production credentials.

What does the audit look for?

Current checks focus on missing error handling, retry/idempotency risk, mutating HTTP requests, silent filter skips, concurrency/ordering risk, recovery settings, observability trade-offs and exported Make designer warnings.

What does static analysis not prove?

It cannot prove credential validity, runtime data correctness, third-party availability, complete business semantics, legal compliance or that every defect has been found.

Why not just use a generic blueprint linter?

Generic linting is useful. PCFlows is deliberately narrower: reliability, data integrity, recovery, remediation order and verification steps.

Is this just a ChatGPT prompt?

No. The free preflight uses deterministic Make-specific checks, so the same blueprint produces repeatable findings. For the paid audit, AI-assisted interpretation may help prioritize or explain those findings, but deterministic evidence remains the source of truth and unsupported runtime claims are not allowed.
