FAQ

Questions before you share a blueprint.

Do you need access to my Make.com account?

No. The current audit is designed around a sanitized exported scenario blueprint. Production credentials are not required.

Does the free scanner upload my blueprint?

No. The core Reliability Preflight runs locally in your browser and does not upload the blueprint to PCFlows.

What should I remove before sharing a blueprint?

Remove passwords, API keys, tokens, private Make webhook URLs, personal data, customer records and other confidential literals. Never send production credentials.

What does the audit look for?

Current checks focus on missing recovery routes, retry/idempotency risk, silent skips, Resume/Ignore behavior, Commit/Rollback boundaries, concurrency, incomplete-execution/data-loss settings and observability trade-offs.

Is this just a ChatGPT prompt?

No. The evidence layer uses deterministic Make-specific checks. AI-assisted interpretation may help prioritize or explain those findings, but it does not replace the underlying evidence and may not invent runtime facts.

Why not use a generic blueprint analyzer?

Generic structural analysis is useful. PCFlows is narrower: business-data integrity, failure semantics, recovery, remediation order and verification.

What can static analysis not tell me?

It cannot prove credential validity, third-party availability, live data correctness, complete business semantics, legal compliance or that every defect has been found.

What if the free scan is enough?

Then PCFlows should say so. The paid audit is intended for workflows where context materially changes how reliability findings should be prioritized and verified.

When do you call an issue fixed?

Not when a warning merely disappears. PCFlows distinguishes detected, statically cleared, evidence-supported and independently verified fixed states. Runtime-dependent issues require their rule-specific synthetic execution checks before the strongest status is used. See the verification standard.
