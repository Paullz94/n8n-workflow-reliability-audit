Privacy & service information

Free local scanner

The core Reliability Preflight runs in the browser. PCFlows does not receive the blueprint during the core scan. Customers should still sanitize blueprints and remove credentials, secrets and personal data.

Audit service

The paid pilot is a fixed-scope technical review of a sanitized Make.com blueprint. Production credentials are not required and should not be sent.

Data shared by email or checkout

If you contact PCFlows or later purchase an audit, information you voluntarily provide may be processed to answer the request, deliver the service, keep required business records, and handle support. Payment information is processed by Stripe; PCFlows does not need your full card details.

Delivery & refunds

The service is delivered asynchronously as a digital report. If PCFlows cannot perform the agreed audit because supplied material is outside the documented scope, the order will be cancelled or refunded before substantive delivery begins. After a customized audit report has been delivered, refunds are assessed case-by-case for material non-delivery or clear failure to provide the described service.

Scope limitations

PCFlows provides technical static analysis and reliability guidance. It does not provide legal, accounting, tax, security certification or compliance certification. Static analysis cannot guarantee runtime correctness, third-party availability, credential validity, data quality, business outcomes, or the absence of defects.

Independent service

PCFlows is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Make.com.
