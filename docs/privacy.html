Privacy & service information

Send less data, not more.

PCFlows is designed around sanitized blueprints and a deliberately small amount of business context.

Free local scanner

The core Reliability Preflight runs in the browser. PCFlows does not receive the blueprint during the core scan.

Local intake generator

The audit-intake form also runs locally and downloads a JSON file to your device. It contains only the fields shown in the form and is not submitted automatically.

Paid audit input

The paid pilot uses a sanitized Make.com blueprint plus a small non-sensitive context file. Production credentials are not required and should not be sent.

Stripe checkout data

When checkout is activated, Stripe processes payment and billing information. The prepared checkout also asks for a scenario name and whether the purchase is made as an individual or business/professional customer. PCFlows does not need your full card details.

Email and delivery data

If you contact PCFlows or send audit files after payment, PCFlows may process your email address, Stripe order reference, sanitized blueprint, intake context and support correspondence to deliver the service, perform the included re-scan, keep required business records and handle support/refunds.

Data minimization

Do not send production credentials, passwords, API keys, private webhook URLs, customer records or unnecessary personal data. A possible secret-like finding stops paid processing until a sanitized copy is supplied.

Independent service

PCFlows is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Make.com.
