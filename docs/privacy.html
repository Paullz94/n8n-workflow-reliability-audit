Privacy & service information

Support: PCMotionstudios@gmail.com

Free local scanner

The core Reliability Preflight runs in the browser. PCFlows does not receive the blueprint during the core scan. Customers should still sanitize blueprints and remove credentials, secrets and personal data.

Local intake generator

The audit-intake form also runs locally and downloads a JSON file to the customer's device. It does not submit the form to PCFlows. The intake is deliberately limited to non-sensitive business context such as scenario name, business goal, critical side effects, ordering requirements and recovery expectations.

Audit service

The paid pilot is a fixed-scope technical review of a sanitized Make.com blueprint. Production credentials are not required and should not be sent.

Stripe checkout data

When paid checkout is activated, Stripe processes the payment and checkout information. The prepared PCFlows checkout asks for accurate billing details, a scenario name, and whether the purchase is made as an individual or a business/professional customer. Stripe can also collect a tax ID when the customer provides one. PCFlows does not need or receive full payment-card details.

Data shared by email

If a customer contacts PCFlows or sends audit files after payment, PCFlows may process the email address, Stripe order reference, sanitized blueprint, non-sensitive intake context and support correspondence to deliver the service, provide the included re-scan, keep required business records and handle refunds/support.

Data minimization

Do not send production credentials, passwords, API keys, private webhook secrets, customer records or unnecessary personal data. PCFlows' fulfillment process is designed to hard-stop when a possible secret-like literal is detected and to exclude the raw customer blueprint from the generated delivery ZIP.

Delivery & refunds

The service is delivered asynchronously as a digital report. If PCFlows cannot perform the agreed audit because supplied material is outside the documented scope, the order will be cancelled or refunded before substantive delivery begins. After a customized audit report has been delivered, refunds are assessed case-by-case for material non-delivery or clear failure to provide the described service. Mandatory statutory rights remain unaffected.

Scope limitations

PCFlows provides technical static analysis and reliability guidance. It does not provide legal, accounting, tax, security certification or compliance certification. Static analysis cannot guarantee runtime correctness, third-party availability, credential validity, data quality, business outcomes, or the absence of defects.

Independent service

PCFlows is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Make.com.
