Realistic failure patterns

Problems businesses actually notice.

Double contact

A webhook retries after a timeout. Make creates the CRM contact again and the customer receives two confirmation messages.

Lost paid lead

The form succeeds, but the CRM write fails. The scenario stops and nobody notices that an advertising lead never reached sales.

Follow-up after reply

A prospect replies or books a call, but no explicit stop/handoff state exists, so scheduled follow-up continues anyway.

Wrong owner

Two near-simultaneous routing events overwrite each other and the lead ends up with the wrong salesperson.

Silent filter skip

A filter excludes a valid lead. The Make run is technically successful, but the expected CRM action never happens.

Duplicate appointment work

The same lead event is replayed and downstream task/appointment creation is not idempotent.
