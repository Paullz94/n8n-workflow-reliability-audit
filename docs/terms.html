Service terms

Current status:

Paid checkout is not open while PCFlows completes its Belgian business registration. These terms are prepared for the fixed-scope pilot and take effect for an order when checkout is activated.

1. Service

The PCFlows Make.com Data Integrity Audit is a fixed-scope technical static review of one sanitized Make.com scenario blueprint. The pilot deliverable includes prioritized reliability/data-integrity findings, remediation and verification guidance, and one asynchronous re-scan after remediation.

2. Customer authorization

You must own the submitted blueprint or be authorized to have it reviewed. Do not submit material that you are not permitted to share.

3. Sanitized input only

Production credentials, passwords, API keys, private webhook secrets, personal/customer records, or other confidential payload data are not required and should not be sent. If PCFlows detects a possible secret-like literal, fulfillment may pause until a newly sanitized blueprint is provided.

4. Delivery

Delivery starts after successful payment and receipt of a usable sanitized blueprint plus the PCFlows intake information. The pilot is asynchronous. PCFlows aims to deliver the initial report within five business days after complete usable input is received. If the supplied material is unusable or outside scope, PCFlows will contact the customer rather than silently expanding the service.

5. Included re-scan

One re-scan is included for the same scenario after the customer applies remediation. To keep the pilot bounded, the re-scan should be requested within 30 calendar days of the initial report unless otherwise agreed.

6. Out of scope

The audit does not include production account access, implementation retainers, emergency incident response, legal/tax advice, security or compliance certification, or a guarantee that every defect has been found.

7. Static-analysis limitations

Static analysis cannot prove runtime correctness, credential validity, third-party availability, live data quality, complete business semantics, or business outcomes. Findings are review signals and technical guidance, not certification.

8. Price and payment

The planned pilot price is EUR 149 for the described fixed scope. The Stripe checkout displays the amount payable and collects the billing information needed for payment and business records. Tax treatment follows PCFlows' applicable registration and customer circumstances at the time of sale.

9. Cancellations and refunds

If PCFlows cannot perform the agreed audit because the supplied material is outside the documented scope, the order will be cancelled or refunded before substantive delivery begins. After a customized report has been delivered, refunds are assessed case-by-case for material non-delivery or clear failure to provide the described service. Nothing in these terms removes mandatory statutory rights that apply to a customer.

10. Support

Support is asynchronous via PCMotionstudios@gmail.com. Never send payment-card details or production secrets by email.

11. Independent service

PCFlows is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Make.com.
