1. Service

The PCFlows Make.com Data Integrity Audit is a fixed-scope technical static review of one sanitized Make.com scenario blueprint. The pilot includes prioritized reliability/data-integrity findings, remediation and verification guidance, and one asynchronous re-scan after remediation.

2. Authorization

You must own the submitted blueprint or be authorized to have it reviewed.

3. Sanitized input only

Production credentials, passwords, API keys, private webhook URLs, personal/customer records or confidential payloads are not required and should not be sent. Possible secret-like input can pause fulfillment until a sanitized copy is supplied.

4. Delivery

Delivery starts after successful payment and receipt of a usable sanitized blueprint plus the PCFlows intake information. The pilot is asynchronous and aims for initial delivery within five business days after complete usable input is received.

5. Included re-scan

One re-scan is included for the same scenario after remediation and should normally be requested within 30 calendar days of the initial report.

6. Out of scope

The fixed price does not silently include production account access, unrestricted implementation, emergency incident response, legal/tax advice, security certification or compliance certification.

7. Limitations

Static analysis cannot prove runtime correctness, credential validity, third-party availability, live data quality, complete business semantics or the absence of defects.

8. Price

The planned pilot price is €149 for the documented fixed scope. Applicable tax treatment depends on PCFlows' registration and customer circumstances at the time of sale.

9. Cancellations and refunds

If PCFlows cannot perform the agreed audit because the supplied material falls outside the documented scope, the order will be cancelled or refunded before substantive delivery begins. After a customized report has been delivered, refunds are assessed case-by-case for material non-delivery or clear failure to provide the described service. Mandatory statutory rights remain unaffected.

Support is asynchronous via PCMotionstudios@gmail.com.
