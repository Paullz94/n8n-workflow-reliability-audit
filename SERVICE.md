# Fixed-Scope n8n Workflow Reliability Audit

Status: limited validation pilot. The public repository is synthetic technical proof, not a customer case study or certification.

## Intended buyer

Teams with an existing n8n workflow and a concrete reliability concern: duplicate side effects, missing/unsafe retries, silent success, unauthenticated webhooks, absent error paths, unclear recovery, or an undocumented handoff.

This is not a general “build any AI agent” offer.

## Pilot scopes

### Static audit — target EUR 99

- One exported n8n workflow, up to 30 nodes.
- Static, secret-redacted report plus manual review.
- Ranked findings and a synthetic runtime-test plan.
- No production credentials, production execution, or workflow edits.
- Target delivery: two business days after safe intake and funded scope.

### Reliability retrofit — target EUR 350

- One exported workflow, up to 30 nodes, plus agreed synthetic fixtures.
- Reproduced before-state for the accepted failure/risk.
- Bounded changes covering applicable validation, idempotency, retry/timeout, error routing, and observability.
- Synthetic before/after evidence, rollback notes, handoff, and one revision against written acceptance criteria.
- Exact delivery date follows audit/scope review.

Prices are validation hypotheses and may exclude taxes where legally applicable. The final scope, seller details, currency, platform fee, tax treatment, and funded milestone are disclosed and agreed before any paid work begins.

## Safe intake

Open a public issue with only:

- intended business outcome;
- connected systems (names only);
- current symptom/risk;
- n8n version and hosting type;
- acceptance condition;
- approximate node count.

Do **not** post workflow JSON, URLs with tokens, credentials, API responses, customer/personal data, confidential logs, or screenshots containing them. If the scope appears suitable, a separate funded contract and safe transfer method must be agreed before private files are requested.

## Boundaries

- Static analysis is not production certification.
- No guarantee of uninterrupted operation, regulatory compliance, security, or business results.
- No legal, medical, accounting, or payment-processing advice.
- No production change without written scope, rollback, and client-controlled approval.
- The client must own or be authorized to share and modify the workflow.
- New paid services, subscriptions, or infrastructure are never added without explicit approval.

## Commercial validation rule

The pilot is considered commercially validated only by an unrelated client's funded milestone or settled payment—not stars, repository traffic, compliments, or unpaid test requests.
