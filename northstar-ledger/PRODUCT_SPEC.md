# Product Spec — Northstar Vibe App Preflight Pack

## Buyer

Solo founders and small teams preparing to ship an AI-assisted web app built with tools such as Codex, Cursor, Claude Code, Lovable, Bolt, Replit, or similar workflows.

## Job to be done

“I have a working app, but I do not know what release-critical checks I forgot. Give me a repeatable local process I can run before real users and real payments arrive.”

## Offer

**EUR 39 one-time** for an offline reusable pack.

Bundle contents:

1. START_HERE.md — 60–90 minute route through the pack.
2. PRELAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md — structured release-readiness checklist.
3. ADVERSARIAL_TESTS.md — authorization, data-isolation, auth, upload, error, and abuse tests.
4. PAYMENTS_TEST_MATRIX.md — payment lifecycle scenarios beyond “checkout succeeded”.
5. AI_REMEDIATION_PROMPTS.md — bounded prompts with acceptance criteria for coding agents.
6. ROLLBACK_AND_INCIDENT.md — rollback, secret-exposure, and failed-release worksheets.
7. LAUNCH_DECISION_RECORD.md — explicit go/hold decision record with evidence links.
8. LICENSE.txt — internal-use license; no resale/redistribution.
9. Copy of the local scanner for convenience.

## Differentiation

- Runs locally; no customer repository upload is needed.
- Deterministic scanner uses Python standard library only.
- Explicitly separates automated findings from manual tests.
- Designed around AI-assisted remediation with verification criteria.
- One-time payment rather than a subscription.

## Boundaries

The pack does not provide penetration testing, a professional security audit, legal/privacy/compliance/accessibility/tax certification, or a guarantee that deployment is secure or production-ready.

## Commercial validation

Commercial validation requires at least one settled, unrelated external-customer payment. Interest, stars, downloads, self-purchases, test-mode charges, pending payments, or refunded payments do not qualify.

## Target economics

- Unit price: EUR 39 gross.
- Target: 26 settled external-customer purchases = EUR 1,014 gross.
- Owner capital target: EUR 0.
- Delivery marginal cost: expected approximately EUR 0 besides payment-platform fees.
