# Decision Log

## 2026-09-25 — Preserve n8n as research, not the primary model

The existing n8n analyzer/service remains useful evidence for pricing, acquisition, and reliability research. The new workstream does not rely on n8n Community participation or recurring manual delivery.

## 2026-09-25 — Select local AI-app release preflight product

Reasons:

- broader current demand signal than a single automation platform;
- self-service digital delivery;
- EUR 0 owner capital is feasible;
- local/no-upload positioning avoids hosted infrastructure;
- a free lite scanner can demonstrate utility while the paid pack adds deeper manual tests and remediation workflow.

Rejected as primary models:

- generic prompt packs: crowded and weakly differentiated;
- manual code-audit service: higher price but owner becomes recurring operator;
- hosted scanner SaaS: hosting/ops cost before validation;
- paid ads: conflicts with the EUR 0 default before willingness-to-pay evidence.

## 2026-09-25 — Checkout strategy revised: Payhip + Stripe for validation

Initial candidate was Lemon Squeezy because its Merchant-of-Record model reduces sales-tax administration. Current platform research found a lower-friction validation advantage in Payhip:

- Free Forever plan: EUR/USD 0 monthly platform cost, with transaction fee on sales.
- Automatic digital-file delivery.
- Stripe can be connected as the payment processor.
- Payhip exposes paid and refunded webhooks.
- Marketplace eligibility begins after at least USD 10 in genuine sales and store review; self-purchases do not count.

That means one genuine EUR 39 sale can cross the published marketplace sales threshold, while Lemon Squeezy requires at least 3 sales or USD 50 before marketplace eligibility.

Phase 1 primary: Payhip + Stripe.
Fallback: Lemon Squeezy if Payhip/Stripe setup or tax/compliance fit is unsuitable.

Identity, payment-processor onboarding, payout destination, tax/business representation, and binding terms remain owner-gated.
