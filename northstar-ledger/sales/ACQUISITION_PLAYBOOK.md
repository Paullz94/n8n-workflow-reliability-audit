# Acquisition Playbook — EUR 0 Owner Capital

## Principle

Acquisition must be organic, targeted, and platform-compliant. Do not mass-post, duplicate content, bypass moderation, purchase fake engagement, or impersonate users.

## Funnel

1. Free local scanner demonstrates utility without requiring an account.
2. Public sample report shows output before purchase.
3. Product listing explains the deeper manual/process pack.
4. Hosted checkout delivers the paid ZIP automatically.
5. Marketplace listing is enabled only after meeting the provider's genuine-sales eligibility threshold.

## Free channels in priority order

1. GitHub discovery for the free scanner and sample report.
2. Direct answers in communities only where self-promotion is explicitly allowed and directly relevant to a user asking about launch readiness.
3. Founder/build-in-public posts that teach a concrete check first and link the free scanner second.
4. Payment-provider marketplace once eligibility is reached.
5. Referral/affiliate program funded only from business-generated revenue if supported.

## Qualification

Good fit: working AI-assisted web app, pre-launch/early paid users, and a buyer wanting a repeatable self-check rather than outsourced auditing.

Bad fit: penetration-test/certification expectations, high-risk domains requiring specialist review, or continuous operator expectations.

## Measurement

Track only provider-verified events and public funnel signals: free scanner usage where verifiable, product-page visits, checkout starts, settled purchases, refunds/chargebacks, and support requests.

Do not count stars, likes, self-testing, pending transactions, or refunded transactions as revenue validation.
