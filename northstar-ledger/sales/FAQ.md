# Customer FAQ

## Does my source code get uploaded?

No. The included scanner runs locally and makes no network calls. The checklists and prompts are local files.

## Does a clean report mean my app is secure?

No. Static heuristics can miss important issues. The pack intentionally includes manual tests and explicit limitations.

## Which stacks are supported?

The scanner recognizes common patterns in JavaScript/TypeScript, Python, configuration files, SQL, Firebase-related files, and general web-app repositories. The manual checks are stack-independent.

## Is this a security audit?

No. It is a release-readiness toolkit for self-checking your own app. Use a qualified security professional when your risk, customer requirements, or regulatory obligations require one.

## Is it a subscription?

No. The planned product is a one-time EUR 39 purchase.

## Can I use it on multiple projects?

Yes, for projects you or your organization own/control, subject to the included license. Redistribution/resale of the paid pack itself is not allowed.

## Can I use the scanner in CI?

Yes. It supports a non-zero exit status when findings at or above a chosen severity are present.
