# Project Northstar Ledger — Operating Goal

Updated: 2026-09-25

## North-star outcome

Build PCFlows into a durable, increasingly autonomous, profitable business. **EUR 1,000 payment-provider-verified gross revenue from real external customers is the first minimum validation milestone, not the end goal.** There is no artificial revenue ceiling.

Financial defaults:
- owner reserve: EUR 500, kept uninjected;
- owner capital injected: EUR 0;
- business-generated cash is tracked separately;
- revenue is reinvested before owner capital is considered;
- owner, test, pending, refunded, chargeback/fictitious transactions never count toward the target.

Owner capital may only be proposed after demonstrated market interest, documented failure of free/cheaper alternatives, a measurable return hypothesis, the minimum exact amount required, and Paul's explicit prior approval.

## Multi-channel requirement

Northstar must not depend on a single website, community, marketplace, or acquisition mechanism.

Validation and acquisition should be diversified across independent channels where the rules permit it. Current research pool includes:
- Make Community / Hire Help;
- Upwork;
- Fiverr;
- Contra;
- Zapier Community / expert ecosystem;
- GitHub organic discovery;
- Reddit as research/community participation only where subreddit rules permit;
- additional directories or marketplaces only after their rules are checked.

No channel is entitled to traffic. A channel is retained only while it produces compliant buyer signal, qualified conversations, or measurable conversions.

## Human, realistic operating tempo

Northstar optimizes for durable trust, not maximum activity.

Rules:
- no bulk outreach, mass-DMs, scraped mailing, duplicate posts, second accounts, moderation bypass, CAPTCHA bypass, or platform-rule evasion;
- no automation whose purpose is to imitate a human in order to evade anti-abuse systems;
- activity must remain plausible for a careful solo operator: small batches, channel-native interaction, individualized context, and pauses between actions;
- do not launch many profiles/listings/posts at once merely because tooling makes it possible;
- prefer one well-prepared interaction over many weak ones;
- do not repeatedly contact the same prospect after non-response;
- respect explicit marketplace/community posting and commercial-solicitation rules;
- where autonomous commercial participation is not clearly permitted, use the channel for research/validation only;
- acquisition automation, when eventually used, must be transparent, permissioned where required, and compliant rather than covert.

The aim is to avoid both spam behavior and unrealistic growth patterns, not to conceal automation.

## Autonomy requirement

The end model must support acquisition, qualification, pricing, checkout, delivery, support, measurement, and iteration without requiring Paul as the daily operator.

A short manual pilot is acceptable only when it:
- validates willingness-to-pay;
- uses EUR 0 owner capital;
- can be executed mostly by Codex/automation;
- is time-bounded;
- has a clear path to automation.

## Continuation / stop conditions

Reaching EUR 1,000 is **not** a stop condition. It records Milestone 1 commercial validation and the operator must continue toward repeatability, higher cumulative revenue, stronger monthly run-rate, repeat purchases and channel diversification.

Continue autonomous work until one of these occurs:
1. a genuine legal, safety, KYC, tax, banking, contractual, identity/account or non-delegable financial blocker requires Paul;
2. a documented failure/pivot gate shows the current wedge is not viable and a bounded pivot decision is required;
3. Paul explicitly pauses or ends the workstream.

Do not stop merely because one revenue milestone, website, listing, or acquisition route has been reached or underperforms.

Canonical revenue milestones are defined in `make-reliability/REVENUE_MILESTONES.md`.
