# Clay Prospecting Operating Policy

Updated: 2026-09-25

Clay is connected and available to PCFlows.

## Purpose

Clay is used to build a high-quality B2B prospect/partner research layer without turning acquisition into bulk spam.

Target archetypes:
- Make/n8n/Zapier automation agencies;
- RevOps/CRM automation consultancies;
- AI automation agencies;
- firms delivering repeatable client workflows;
- operations consultancies where independent release QA could reduce handover risk.

## Current autonomous use

Allowed now:
- search companies from public data;
- search founders/heads/directors at relevant firms;
- enrich professional role/company context;
- score PCFlows fit;
- watch company news/job openings as buying signals;
- keep a small shortlist inside Clay;
- feed anonymized/company-level learnings back into product positioning.

Not allowed during the registration pause:
- sales solicitation;
- checkout links;
- paid service promises;
- cold email.

## Post-launch channel strategy

Even after the legal launch gate:
- inbound/job-post demand remains first priority;
- Clay partner outreach only begins after the direct-marketing compliance gate is separately satisfied;
- at most a very small number of highly tailored contacts per day;
- no repeated chase sequence without a meaningful reason;
- one objection permanently suppresses future direct-marketing outreach.

## Current research observations

Clay already surfaced plausible agency/AI-service archetypes where independent reliability QA could become a partner product:
- firms selling AI/workflow automation;
- agencies selling revenue automation/lead routing;
- AI product firms with customer-success/technology strategy leadership.

Search quality is imperfect, so company similarity is only a first-pass signal. A prospect must still be manually/agent-validated against its public website/services before any future outreach.

## Data policy

Do not commit prospect personal emails, phone numbers, private enrichment values or detailed person profiles to the public GitHub repository.

Clay remains the working prospect store. GitHub stores only rules, aggregate learnings and non-personal operating policy.
