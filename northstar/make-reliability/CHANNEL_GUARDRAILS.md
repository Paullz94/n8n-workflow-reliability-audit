# Channel Guardrails

Policy check: 2026-09-25

Northstar uses each acquisition channel according to that channel's own rules. "Human pace" means restrained, relevant activity — never attempts to disguise automation or evade anti-abuse systems.

## Make Community — research only for autonomous operation

Current official community guidelines state:
- promotion belongs only in Hire a Pro;
- Offer Help permits one service-listing topic per person;
- promotional posts/DMs outside designated areas are prohibited;
- AI-generated responses are prohibited;
- automated content is prohibited;
- duplicate content, multiple accounts and ban evasion are prohibited.

Northstar decision:
- autonomous ChatGPT/Codex activity on Make Community is **research-only**;
- do not autonomously post replies, DMs, service listings or promotional content there;
- if Paul later wants a listing, Paul must author the actual original community text himself and submit it under his legitimate account.

Sources:
- https://community.make.com/guidelines
- https://community.make.com/t/want-to-offer-your-services-promote-yourself-in-offer-help/96842

## Upwork — platform-contained only

Current Upwork guidance requires pre-contract communication to remain on Upwork and prohibits off-platform payment/circumvention.

Northstar decision:
- use Upwork research now;
- future proposals only through Paul's legitimate account after any required profile/KYC setup;
- no email/phone/payment links in pre-contract outreach;
- no attempt to move a lead to Stripe or another checkout when the lead originated under an Upwork contract path that requires Upwork payment;
- no proposal automation intended to exceed or evade platform limits.

Sources:
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360052511133-Circumvention-and-why-it-s-against-the-rules
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360052511833-Get-to-know-each-other-before-a-contract

## Fiverr — marketplace-contained only

Current Fiverr policy requires business/payment to stay on Fiverr; third-party communication is generally permitted only after an order is placed.

Northstar decision:
- use Fiverr for pricing/packaging research now;
- a future Gig must be operated through Paul's legitimate seller account;
- never direct Fiverr users to external checkout;
- no unsolicited off-platform contact;
- no external payment links in a Gig or customer messages.

Sources:
- https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/12792122691601-Stay-protected-Fiverr-s-off-platform-policy
- https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37554441398929-Our-Community-Standards

## Contra — direct engagement, no bulk/systematic outreach

Contra's current terms prohibit unsolicited advertising/spam, mass or systematic outreach, scraping, automated limit circumvention and multiple-account evasion. The platform is intended for direct project engagement.

Northstar decision:
- research demand now;
- later use only through a legitimate Paul/business profile if account requirements are satisfied;
- no bulk applications/messages;
- no substantially identical message batches;
- no scraping or contact-list building from Contra;
- no automation to circumvent message/project limits.

Source:
- https://contra.com/policies/terms

## Reddit — subreddit-by-subreddit

Rules vary by community.

Northstar decision:
- research is allowed using public threads;
- before any commercial contribution, read the current subreddit rules;
- no duplicate cross-post campaigns;
- no unsolicited DM campaign;
- if self-promotion is not expressly compatible with the subreddit rules, remain research-only.

## GitHub — technical trust asset

Northstar currently controls this channel directly.

Use:
- public technical documentation;
- synthetic examples;
- deterministic scanner code;
- changelog/status.

Do not:
- manufacture stars/issues/users;
- create fake testimonials;
- spam issues in unrelated repositories;
- mass-mention users;
- claim unverified revenue or customers.

## Direct outreach outside marketplaces

Not active yet.

If later validated:
- only a very small set of clearly relevant recipients;
- individualized reason for contact;
- respect applicable marketing/privacy law and opt-out requirements;
- no scraped mailing lists;
- no repeated follow-up after non-response;
- no automated sender behavior designed to imitate a person.

## Pace gate

At launch, prefer:
- one well-formed listing/profile per permitted platform;
- a small number of relevant applications/interactions at a time;
- observation of response quality before increasing activity;
- no simultaneous "launch everywhere" burst.

Increase activity only after a channel shows legitimate engagement and support capacity remains bounded.
