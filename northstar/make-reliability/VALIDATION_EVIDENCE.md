# Market Validation Evidence

Research snapshot: 2026-09-25

Purpose: preserve dated evidence behind the Make reliability wedge without depending on a single platform.

## Independent buyer-signal channels

### Make Community
Observed:
- active Hire Help posts for Make automation specialists;
- current reliability discussions around error handling, retries, incomplete executions and recovery;
- current examples of scenarios that report success while expected writes do not occur.

Interpretation:
- strong problem-language source;
- community participation must remain rule-compliant and non-spammy;
- use primarily for research unless commercial participation is clearly allowed.

### Upwork
Observed:
- current Make QA/troubleshooting work asking specialists to review scenarios, validate mappings/webhooks/APIs, test edge cases and improve reliability.

Interpretation:
- higher-intent buyer signal than general community discussion;
- any future proposal activity must use Paul's legitimate account and comply with marketplace rules/KYC.

### Fiverr
Observed:
- existing paid Make/n8n audit/fix offers at low entry prices.

Interpretation:
- proves commoditized troubleshooting exists;
- useful floor/packaging benchmark, not a reason to match the lowest price.

### Contra
Observed:
- paid Make/n8n/Zapier automation-specialist roles with production debugging/reliability responsibilities.

Interpretation:
- confirms businesses pay for automation reliability work beyond one marketplace.

### Zapier ecosystem
Observed:
- large troubleshooting corpus and an official expert-hiring path.

Interpretation:
- adjacent expansion market after the Make wedge is validated;
- do not dilute the MVP by building Zapier support yet.

### Reddit
Observed:
- current Make debugging discussions mentioning mappings, breakage and troubleshooting pain.

Interpretation:
- buyer-language/research source;
- commercial participation only where subreddit rules allow it.

## Direct competitor gate

A recent Apify product scans Make blueprints with fixed static rules for cost/security/reliability/correctness. Another scenario-health analyzer also exists.

Decision:
- generic blueprint linting is **not** sufficiently differentiated;
- Northstar narrows to reliability/data integrity, privacy-first local scanning, retry/idempotency, silent-success risk, recovery and remediation ordering.
