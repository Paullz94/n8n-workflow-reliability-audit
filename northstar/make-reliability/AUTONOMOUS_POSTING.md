# Autonomous Posting Policy

Updated: 2026-09-25

Goal: PCFlows content is useful, sparse and autonomous after launch.

## Owned site
A weekly scheduled task is already active.
It publishes at most one evidence-based guide when the legal launch gate is complete.

## Social
Prepared queue: `content_queue.json`.

Target cadence:
- maximum 2 useful text posts per week initially;
- no decorative AI-generated images/icons;
- no engagement bait;
- no fake customer stories;
- no repetitive repost blast.

A social publishing connector is required for true hands-off posting. Current plugin discovery shows Metricool is available for connected-brand analytics/scheduling/post creation, but it is not connected yet.

## Prospect discovery
Clay is available as a prospect discovery/engagement connector and has been surfaced for optional connection.

## Marketplace channels
No direct Upwork/Contra action connector was returned in current plugin discovery.

Therefore full autonomous marketplace application requires either:
- a supported authorized cloud-browser/Work workflow; or
- a future direct connector.

Do not fake this capability with scraping/CAPTCHA bypass/browser impersonation.

## Make Community
Research only for autonomous AI operation while its current rules prohibit AI-generated/automated community content.

The autonomous action on a prohibited channel is to abstain.
