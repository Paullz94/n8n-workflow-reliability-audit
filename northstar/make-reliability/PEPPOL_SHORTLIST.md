# Peppol / E-invoicing Zero-Capital Shortlist

Research date: 2026-09-25

Purpose: prepare a compliant low-cost route for PCFlows before any in-scope Belgian B2B invoice. No account has been opened and no paid subscription is authorized by this document.

## Official baseline

Belgium's e-invoicing portal says businesses need end-user software connected to Peppol for structured e-invoicing and publishes a list of conforming applications as a practical aid. The government explicitly states that listing is **not** a quality endorsement/certification and that some market solutions are partly free or low-cost.

Official source:
https://efactuur.belgium.be/nl/article/softwareoplossingen-voor-het-verzenden-ontvangen-en-verwerken-van-elektronische-facturen

The downloadable government application list is an XLSX and was not machine-readable in the current research tool session. Therefore the candidates below are **vendor-claim shortlist items**, not claims that PCFlows has independently verified their current inclusion in the government XLSX. Verify the official list and current terms immediately before activation.

## Candidate A — Informer

Vendor states:
- Peppol sending and receiving for Belgian businesses;
- free until 1 January 2029 on a fair-use basis;
- no card details required for the free start;
- paid rates after the free period start around EUR 9/month.

Source:
https://www.informer.eu/en-be/

Why it fits the pilot:
- zero owner capital now;
- intended as end-user invoicing software rather than only an API/access point;
- enough for a very low-volume pilot if the fair-use conditions remain suitable.

## Candidate B — Dokus

Vendor states:
- free plan;
- no card;
- Peppol is part of its invoice intake/workflow;
- built and hosted in Belgium.

Source:
https://dokus.tech/en

Why it may fit:
- zero-cost entry;
- broader evidence/bank/bookkeeping workflow could later reduce manual reconciliation.

Open questions before activation:
- exact free-plan limits for outgoing Peppol invoices;
- whether sending **and** receiving are included without a paid tier;
- whether it appears on the current official end-user software list.

## Candidate C — Factuo

Vendor states:
- free version;
- Peppol electronic invoicing;
- designed for Belgian 2026 requirements;
- company/VAT number used during setup.

Source:
https://factuo.be/en/

Open questions:
- exact free-plan sending/receiving limits;
- data export / bookkeeping evidence quality;
- current inclusion in the government end-user list.

## Not selected yet

Do not sign up for multiple Peppol identities/providers simultaneously merely to test them. That can create needless migration/registration friction.

Activation order once the enterprise/VAT number is available:
1. re-check the current Belgian government software list;
2. verify current free-plan terms and Peppol send/receive capability;
3. choose **one** smallest adequate solution;
4. send/receive a compliant test or validation document as supported;
5. only consider paid software after real invoice volume/support burden demonstrates a need.

## Owner-capital gate

Current approved additional software spend: **EUR 0**.

A paid bookkeeping/e-invoicing subscription needs a separate exact-cost justification if no free compliant route is adequate.
