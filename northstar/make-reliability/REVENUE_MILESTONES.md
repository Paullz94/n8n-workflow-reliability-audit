# PCFlows Revenue Milestones

Updated: 2026-09-28

These are operating checkpoints, not ceilings. Reaching a milestone never ends the business workstream.

Only `verified_gross_revenue` under `LEDGER_POLICY.md` counts.

## Commercial checkpoints

### M0 — First qualified external conversation
Definition:
- a real external prospect responds or initiates contact;
- there is a concrete workflow/reliability problem;
- the conversation is commercially relevant.

Action:
- qualify fit;
- preserve the lead;
- if the prospect wants paid work, trigger the owner VAT prerequisite before quote/contract/payment.

### M1 — First verified external payment
Definition:
- first real external provider-verified payment;
- not owner/test/pending/refunded.

Action:
- complete fulfillment cleanly;
- capture actual acquisition source, sales friction, support burden and delivery time.

### M2 — EUR 500 cumulative verified gross
Purpose:
- confirm that the first sale was not a purely isolated event.

Review:
- reply-to-sale conversion;
- average order value;
- best acquisition channel;
- common objections;
- fulfillment bottlenecks.

### M3 — EUR 1,000 cumulative verified gross
Purpose:
- **minimum commercial validation floor**.

Important:
- this is not the end goal;
- do not stop;
- record that external willingness-to-pay has been validated and continue toward repeatability.

### M4 — EUR 2,500 cumulative verified gross
Purpose:
- demonstrate multiple-customer repeatability or meaningful repeat purchase/agency use.

Review:
- whether EUR 79 / EUR 149 / EUR 399 mix should change;
- whether one channel clearly outperforms;
- whether an agency/release-QA motion is emerging.

### M5 — EUR 5,000 cumulative verified gross
Purpose:
- prove a stronger repeatable sales engine.

Review:
- repeat customer share;
- referral signal;
- customer acquisition cost if any;
- margin after refunds/tooling;
- whether business-generated revenue can fund carefully chosen growth experiments.

### M6 — EUR 10,000 cumulative verified gross
Purpose:
- validate that PCFlows can scale beyond a small pilot.

Review:
- monthly run-rate;
- channel concentration risk;
- fulfillment automation rate;
- support hours per EUR of revenue;
- recurring/portfolio demand.

### M7 — EUR 25,000 cumulative verified gross
Purpose:
- establish durable commercial traction.

Review:
- recurring revenue opportunities;
- agency/partner concentration;
- capacity and reliability of autonomous operations;
- whether new products are justified by repeated paid demand.

### M8 — EUR 50,000 cumulative verified gross
Purpose:
- test whether the business has become a durable autonomous revenue system rather than a one-off service experiment.

## Beyond M8

There is no terminal revenue milestone encoded in Northstar.

After EUR 50,000:
- continue recording additional material thresholds;
- prioritize sustainable profit, repeat revenue, customer satisfaction, diversification and autonomy;
- do not expand product scope merely because revenue grew;
- use actual paid demand to decide what to build next.

## Operating principle

The sequence is:

qualified conversation
-> verified first payment
-> repeatability
-> EUR 1,000 minimum validation
-> multi-customer growth
-> stronger monthly run-rate
-> repeat/referral/agency revenue
-> durable autonomous business.

Revenue milestones do not override legal, privacy, platform, customer-safety or owner-capital gates.
