## 2. Service

The PCFlows Make.com Data Integrity Audit is a fixed-scope technical review of a customer-authorized, sanitized Make.com scenario blueprint.

The pilot scope may include:
- deterministic static reliability analysis;
- prioritized findings relating to retries, idempotency, duplicate writes, concurrent execution, silent skips, error handling, recovery and observability;
- remediation and verification guidance;
- one asynchronous re-scan after remediation where included in the purchased offer.

The customer must own the blueprint or be authorized to submit it.

## 3. Customer data and security

The free core scanner runs locally in the user's browser and does not require the blueprint to be uploaded to PCFlows.

## 6. Consumer withdrawal right

For consumer customers purchasing online, Belgian distance-selling rules generally provide a 14-day withdrawal period for services.

If a consumer asks PCFlows to begin performing the audit during that 14-day period, the paid flow must obtain:
1. the consumer's express request to begin performance during the withdrawal period; and
2. acknowledgement that the withdrawal right ends once the service has been fully performed.

If the consumer withdraws after expressly requesting that service performance begin but before full performance, applicable Belgian rules may allow a proportionate charge for work already performed.

Do not enable immediate paid B2C fulfilment until the checkout records the necessary request/acknowledgement.

## 7. Refund / cancellation policy

If PCFlows cannot perform the agreed audit because the supplied material is outside the documented scope, the order will be cancelled or refunded before substantive delivery begins.

After a customized report has been delivered, refund requests are assessed in accordance with applicable mandatory consumer rights and for material non-delivery or clear failure to provide the described service.

Nothing in these terms limits mandatory statutory consumer rights.

- runtime correctness;

- complete business semantics;

- security certification;
