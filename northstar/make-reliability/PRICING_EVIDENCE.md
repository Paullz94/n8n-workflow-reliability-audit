## Interpretation

EUR 149 is not obviously outside the current visible market band for a focused reliability audit.

PCFlows should not compete primarily on being cheapest. The intended value at EUR 149 is:
- Make-specific deterministic evidence;
- privacy-first static intake;
- business-impact prioritization;
- prevent/detect/recover remediation;
- verification checklist;
- one re-scan;
- no production credentials.

## Pricing experiment rules

Keep EUR 149 unchanged for the first genuine WTP test unless:
- qualified prospects repeatedly reject the price specifically;
- delivery burden proves materially higher/lower than expected;
- the scope changes.

Do not discount preemptively before real objections exist.

Possible later experiments only after evidence:
- EUR 99 narrower audit if price is the main blocker;
- EUR 199+ if paid delivery repeatedly requires more manual interpretation than the pilot scope allows;
- portfolio tier only when multiple-scenario demand appears.

Do not use fake scarcity, crossed-out prices or fabricated customer counts.
