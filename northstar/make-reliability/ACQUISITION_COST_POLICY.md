# Acquisition Cost Policy

Updated: 2026-09-25

Northstar's default remains EUR 0 owner capital for acquisition.

## Upwork

Current official Upwork information:
- joining as a freelancer is free;
- job proposals use Connects;
- additional Connects cost USD 0.15 each;
- free monthly/earned Connects may be available depending on account/offer;
- freelancer service fee is shown per contract and can range from 0% to 15%;
- Project Catalog lets clients discover/purchase a pre-scoped project without the freelancer manually bidding on every job.

Operational rule:
- do **not** purchase Connects with owner capital during the initial validation cycle;
- if a legitimate free Connects allocation exists, use it only on the top-ranked P1 opportunities;
- never boost proposals during the first cycle;
- prefer Project Catalog as the inbound Upwork surface because it does not require repeatedly spending proposal Connects;
- platform service fees deducted from real business revenue are operating expenses, not owner-capital acquisition spend.

Official references:
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062898-Understanding-and-using-Connects
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062538-Learn-about-the-Freelancer-Service-Fee
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058234233-How-to-get-started-with-Project-Catalog-as-a-freelancer

## Contra

Current official Contra information:
- contractors can create a profile, set up payments, send proposals and find work on the free plan;
- Contra markets contractor earnings as commission-free;
- current free-plan platform/payment fees are generally charged to the paying client rather than deducted as a freelancer marketplace commission;
- paid Contra Pro is optional and is not justified before real demand.

Operational rule:
- Free plan only;
- no Pro upgrade with owner capital;
- publish one fixed PCFlows service after registration;
- no bulk/systematic outreach;
- keep all platform-originated work within the required platform/payment rules.

Official references:
- https://help.contra.com/en/articles/9322412-how-to-add-services-to-your-contra-profile
- https://contra.com/pricing
- https://contra.com/commission-free

## Direct PCFlows site

- EUR 0 acquisition spend;
- no paid ads during first validation cycle;
- use free scanner, technical guide and organic discovery;
- Stripe fee is a transaction expense funded by actual revenue.

## Make Community

- research only under the current no-AI/no-automated-content community rule;
- no autonomous AI-generated sales post/DM;
- no attempt to disguise generated content as human-written community participation.

## Spend gate

Before any acquisition spend:
1. demonstrate a real high-fit opportunity;
2. use all free/platform-native alternatives first;
3. state exact minimum cost;
4. state expected measurable benefit;
5. get explicit Paul approval.

No speculative paid ads, subscriptions, proposal boosts or marketplace upgrades.
